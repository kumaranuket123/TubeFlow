TubeFlow

TubeFlow is an Android app that detects videos you record with your camera and uploads them straight to your own YouTube channel as private (or unlisted) videos, then adds them to a playlist you choose.

  • Automatic detection of new camera videos
  • Resumable uploads in the background, with Wi-Fi / 5G and charging rules
  • Playlist selection and management
  • No server, no ads, no tracking: your videos go only to YouTube
